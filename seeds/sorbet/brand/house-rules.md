# Sorbet rules (on top of the studio rules)

Sorbet's films are **show-offs**. They exist to stop a thumb mid-scroll and make motion designers say "how did they do that?". Spectacle first; restraint is the failure mode here. The critic treats any violation of the "Banned" list as **blocking** — and here the worst violation is being *tasteful and forgettable*.

## Banned (immediate rejection)
- A calm shot: any 1.5 s window where nothing big changes (scale, colour, camera or rhythm).
- Polite motion: small moves, gentle fades, "corporate" critical springs everywhere, content politely sliding 24 px.
- Opacity fade as the only entrance or exit; cross-dissolves.
- Empty frames: a few small elements floating in a big empty background. Fill the frame, then break it.
- Decorative gradients, gradient text, lens flares, chromatic aberration, added film grain, stock "AI magic" (brains, circuits, sparkles).
- Generic particles. Particles are allowed only when they are **brand matter**: sprinkles, drips, splats, scoop crumbs, wafer shards.
- Text that can't be read when it matters: spectacle never costs the hook or the final lockup.

## Allowed and encouraged (wins over any studio lesson that says otherwise)
- **Overshoot, squash and stretch, jelly physics**: underdamped springs, elastic landings, wobble on impact. Ice cream is soft — make it feel soft.
- **Smash cuts, hard cuts on the beat, whip pans, speed ramps, match cuts** between shapes (a scoop becomes a planet becomes an "o").
- **Giant type**: words that fill or overflow the frame, cropped by the edges, kinetic typography, letters as objects.
- **Camera**: aggressive push-ins and pull-outs, 3D perspective (CSS 3D / canvas projection), rotation, fast dolly zooms; short impact shake (≤ 120 ms) on heavy landings.
- **Full-frame colour floods** and colour-blocked frames; up to 3 colour cuts per second on a beat (never sustained strobing).
- Drips, melts, splats, pours, sticky stretches — liquid and soft-body behaviour.

## Structure
- Spectacle never costs the hook or the final lockup: the last frame is the one calm moment, earned.

## Sound
- Music drives the cut: hits, risers, drops. Big impacts get big sounds; silence is a weapon (one beat of silence before the drop). Commercially licensed music only.
