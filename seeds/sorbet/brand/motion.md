# Sorbet — motion rules (spectacular)

The previous, restrained doctrine is archived in `seeds/sorbet/brand-classic/`. This one is deliberately loud: Sorbet films are demos of what motion can do. `DESIGN.md` still defines the palette, the type and the logo; `brand/house-rules.md` replaces the studio house rules for this project.

## Stance

**Sugar rush.** Sorbet is cold, sweet, soft and bright: every film should feel like a brain freeze — a jolt, a rush, then a sweet landing. Maximal energy, total control. Chaos is choreographed to the frame.

## Direction

- **Tempo**: something big every 0.5–1.5 s, locked to the music (≈ 120–128 BPM: a beat every 0.47–0.5 s). Build → drop → release.
- **Scale extremes**: go from a full-frame macro (a single sprinkle, the curve of a letter) to a wide shot in one move. Never stay at "medium".
- **Fill the frame**: big shapes, giant type, colour floods edge to edge. Then break it: crop, split, smash.
- **Transitions are events**: a scoop lands and splats into the next scene's background colour; a letter's counter becomes a portal you fly through; a drip falls and cuts the frame in two. Hard cuts on beats are welcome.
- **Physics with personality**: underdamped springs (overshoot 10–25 %), squash & stretch on landings (up to 30 %), jelly wobble that settles in 0.3–0.5 s. Use springs with low damping, or custom tweens with back/elastic easing.
- **Camera**: real moves — 3D perspective, rotation, dolly zoom, whip pan with heavy motion blur, a short shake on heavy impacts.

## Palette in motion

- Raspberry `#ff4f79`, mango `#ffb02e`, mint `#2fd3ae`, lilac `#b9a6ff`, plum_night `#120e22`, cream `#fff7ee`.
- **Colour blocking**: full-frame floods, hard colour cuts on beats, split screens in the three scoop colours. Flat colour, no gradients.
- Plum_night is the "freezer"; cream is the "melt". Go back and forth.

## Type in motion

- Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold, **huge**: 300–900 px in 1080p, cropped by the frame, tracking −0.04 em.
- Kinetic: words punch in on beats (scale 0 → 115 % → 100 %), letters split, stretch, drip, stack; a word can be the set the camera flies through.
- Readability where it matters: the hook and the final lockup are held long enough to read (≥ 0.35 s per word + 0.5 s).

## Brand matter (the only particles allowed)

Sprinkles (short rounded rectangles in the five colours), drips and splats (soft blobby SVG paths), scoop crumbs, wafer shards (`wafer` `#f1e6d8` with 2 px `plum_ink` cross-hatch). Use them in bursts on impacts, never as ambient glitter.

## Logo

Built from its layers in `brand/logo/*.svg`. The scoops can fall from the sky, splat, bounce and wobble before they settle; the wordmark can punch in letter by letter on beats. The final lockup is the one calm moment: still, centred, held ≥ 1.5 s.

## Don'ts

Polite motion · empty frames · fades · gradients · lens flares · RGB split · generic particles · stock "AI magic" · copy like "10x", "Powered by AI", "The future of…".
